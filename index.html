Honey Bee Microbiota Krona Charts

These interactive Krona charts show the bacterial taxa found in honey bee samples, drawn as nested rings from phylum at the center out to species. Slice size shows relative abundance; tick "Color by % Prevalence" to shade each taxon from red (rare) to green (widespread). The data are the public 16S rRNA gene sequence libraries compiled for Anderson and Copeland (2024), The honey bee "hive" microbiota: meta-analysis reveals a native and aerobic microbiota prevalent throughout the social resource niche, Frontiers in Bee Science. The charts themselves are not part of the published article.

  • Bee gut and whole-bee libraries

    Workers, nurses, foragers, and queens, with separate views for gut regions; pick a group from the dataset menu at the top left.

  • Hive and colony resource niches

    Honey, beebread, larvae, royal jelly, and the worker crop and hypopharyngeal glands, the shared food and feeding sites of the colony.

Each chart is a single self-contained HTML file (about 3 MB and 1 MB). They work on a phone but are easiest to explore on a larger screen. Click a slice to zoom in; click the center to zoom out.
